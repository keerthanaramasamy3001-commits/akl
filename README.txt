AK REALITY CORE - WEBSITE

HOW TO VIEW
Extract the whole ZIP, then double-click index.html.

FILES
index.html      page content
css/style.css   design
js/script.js    menu, slider, video popup, enquiry form (settings at the top)
images/         original logo and photos
fonts/          Gelasio and Open Sans, with their licences

BEFORE GOING LIVE
1. Phone, email, address: confirm them and edit in index.html (also set the email in js/script.js).
2. Numbers (500+, 1M+ Sq.Ft, 1000+): taken from the design image. Publish only true figures.
3. Social links and video: add them at the top of js/script.js. They do nothing until added.
4. Draft text to review: Our Services, service menu items, Our Approach, and the
   headlines on slides 2 and 3. "Quality Assurance" and "Trusted Partner" come from the design.
5. Projects section: replace "coming soon" with real project names and photos.
6. Photos were cut from the design image, so they are low resolution and may look soft,
   and they are illustrations. Replace them with high-resolution photos you have the right
   to use (keep the same file names). All 3 slides use the same hero photo.
7. Enquiry form: opens the visitor's email app. For direct sending, connect a form service or server.

LOGO
images/ak-reality-core-logo.jpg is your file, unaltered. CSS only hides the blank white border.
